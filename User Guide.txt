KARIUSS MAX HEADTRACKING 1.8.0 — ENGLISH QUICK GUIDE

1. Extract the entire Windows ZIP. Keep _internal beside the .exe.
2. Open Kariuss Max Headtracking.exe. No Python or separate OpenTrack app is required.
3. Choose English in the top-right dropdown if necessary.
4. Connect Rokid Max using a USB connection that carries data.
5. Place the glasses on a flat surface for 6 seconds to calibrate.
6. Put on the glasses, look straight ahead, and click Reset view or press F8.
7. Close OpenTrack if it is running. Click Connect to game, open the game, and enter the cockpit.
8. If tracking is not detected, leave the Kariuss game connection enabled and restart the game.

GAME BUTTON COLORS
Green: the game has registered and a receiving component is loaded in a running process.
Red: connection is enabled but no game is connected, or the connection failed.
Gray: connection is disabled. Click the same button again to toggle it.

ADJUSTMENTS
Smoothing: Off / Light / Medium / Strong. Medium is the default.
Sensitivity: 80% is the default. Reduce it if turning feels too sensitive.
Output rate: 44.4 / 50 / 80 / 100 / 140 / 200 Hz. Default: 100 Hz.
Invert each axis if a turn or tilt runs in the wrong direction.
Pause freezes the game's view while the model still moves. Resume resets the center.

BUTTONS ON THE GLASSES
Choose extra actions under Glasses buttons. Settings are saved automatically.
The glasses' original volume and brightness functions still operate.
Use F8, or two opposite volume presses within 0.8 seconds, to reset without changing brightness.
At maximum volume press down then up; at minimum volume press up then down.

BACKGROUND AND RECONNECTION
Unplugging and reconnecting USB is detected automatically unless you selected Disconnect.
Allow 6 seconds to calibrate again after reconnection.
Closing the window with X keeps tracking active in the system tray near the Windows clock.
Double-click the glasses tray icon or launch the app again to reopen the window.
Choose Quit app to stop completely.

LANGUAGE AND LOGS
The English / Tiếng Việt dropdown changes labels, status, visible logs, and tray menus immediately.
Your language choice is remembered. Tracking settings are preserved.
Activity timestamps use UTC+7: HH:mm:ss DD/MM/YYYY.
Stored logs retain the language used when each line was recorded.
Settings and logs are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%/Kariuss Max Headtracking.

UPDATING
Quit the app, extract the new Windows ZIP into a new folder, and open the new executable.
Existing user preferences are reused. There is no automatic updater.

SCOPE
Tested tracking hardware/game: Rokid Max and Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.
Tracks three rotations; position X/Y/Z is zero. Press F8 if the heading drifts.
Video on the glasses needs a separate video connection; data-only USB-C provides no picture.
An adapter must carry both video and USB data to preserve tracking.
Real FPV camera outputs are not implemented yet.
