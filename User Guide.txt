# Kariuss Max Headtracking

English · Tiếng Việt (README.md)

Head tracking and mouse control with Rokid Max on Windows. Connect directly to games without running OpenTrack separately. Head tracking has been tested with Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024; other games and glasses need separate verification.

Version 1.10.5 — English / Vietnamese. This guide and its screenshots show this version. Screenshots use the Vietnamese interface; select English in the app to use the English labels shown in these instructions.

## Download and open the app

Open Releases (https://github.com/KhangNguyen1307/Rokid-max-Headtracking/releases), download the Windows x64 package, extract the entire archive, and open Kariuss Max Headtracking.exe. Keep the _internal folder beside the executable.

## Connect video and head tracking together

### Method 1 Computer with USB C video output

Use the original glasses cable to connect Rokid Max directly to a laptop or desktop USB-C port that provides video, power, and USB data. Port specifications commonly call this DisplayPort over USB-C or DisplayPort Alt Mode. A charging-only or data-only port will not display video in the glasses.

### Method 2 Computer without USB C video output

Use an HDMI to USB-C adapter for AR glasses that also passes USB data, such as the matching Hagibis version pictured below. Connect all three paths:

1. HDMI number 1 → computer. On a desktop with a dedicated graphics card, connect it to that card's HDMI output.
2. USB power and data number 2 → a USB port on the same computer. Use a data-capable cable. A wall charger supplies power only and cannot send head motion back to the PC.
3. Glasses USB-C output number 3 → Rokid Max, using the glasses cable. Choose the correct HDMI to USB-C direction; a common USB-C to HDMI adapter is not a substitute.

In Windows Settings → System → Display, select Duplicate to show the same picture on both screens, or Extend and move the game to the glasses display.

## Connect and calibrate the glasses

1. Open the app and select English or Tiếng Việt. Connect the glasses; Kariuss detects them automatically. If the connection was disabled, click Connect glasses. Use Rescan USB if the device is missing.
2. Leave the glasses still on a flat surface for 6 seconds, following the app's message. Then put them on, look straight ahead, and click Reset view or press F8.

The glasses button is green when connected, red when searching without a connection or when an error occurs, and gray when disabled. Once connected, its label changes to Disconnect. Unplugging and reconnecting the cable works automatically while the connection remains enabled.

*Select the glasses and check their connection at the top of the window. This screenshot shows the state with no glasses attached.*

## Play simulator games with Headtracking

1. Keep the default Headtracking (Simulator) mode. Close OpenTrack if it is running.
2. Click Connect to game, open your game, and enter the cockpit. In Flight Simulator 2024, use the ordinary monitor mode.
3. When the game button turns green and the game name appears, look straight ahead, press F8, and try turning your head. Red means no game is detected or an error occurred; gray means the connection is disabled.

If the game does not respond, enable head tracking or TrackIR in its settings if available. Try reopening the game while Kariuss's game connection is enabled.

*Select Headtracking, then click Connect to game. Sensitivity and smoothing are directly below. The values in the screenshot are examples.*

## Frequently used controls

- Reset using the glasses: look straight ahead and press volume up then down, or down then up, within 0.8 seconds. This is the default action; change it in the Glasses buttons tab. The buttons still change the glasses' volume or brightness as usual.
- Pause and resume: use the corresponding button to temporarily stop controlling the view with your head.
- Invert: toggle the relevant checkbox if left/right, up/down, or head tilt is reversed.
- Sensitivity and smoothing: lower sensitivity if turning is too fast; increase smoothing if the view shakes. For head tracking, start with 80% and Medium. Initially keep Output rate at 100 Hz.
- Background operation: click × to hide the window. Reopen it from the Kariuss icon near the Windows clock, possibly inside the ^ menu. Select Quit app to stop completely.

## Play FPS games with Mouse Control

1. Select Mouse Control (FPS). The selected mode turns green; click the other mode to switch directly.
2. Click Click to start mouse control, then switch to your game or desktop to try it. While the Kariuss window is in front, mouse output pauses so you can adjust settings. Click Stop mouse control to stop.
3. Open the Mouse control tab and choose a control style:

- Hold a key to recenter your head: choose a key unused by your game. When your head has turned too far, hold that key, face forward again, then release it to continue turning, like lifting and repositioning a mouse.

*In the Mouse control tab, choose the key-hold style and the key used to recenter your head.*

- Hold your head turned for continuous turning: within the chosen angle, the mouse follows your head normally. Beyond the threshold, keep your head turned to continue rotating the view. Return within the threshold to stop continuous turning. Adjust the start angle and turn speed in this tab.

*For continuous turning, set the start angle and turn speed here.*

Reset mouse or F8 centers the pointer and resets the glasses reference and displayed angles to zero while motion data is available. It does not force an FPS game's camera back to its original direction in the map.

## If the glasses are dark or motion is missing

The app detects the glasses but they are dark: check the video connection and Windows display settings. The glasses show video but the app does not detect them: check the adapter's USB data path and its cable back to the PC. Read the messages in the Activity log at the bottom of the app.

## Source and building

See BUILDING_EN.md (BUILDING_EN.md) for running, testing, and packaging on Windows x64 with Python 3.12. Dependency sources and replacement instructions are listed in THIRD_PARTY_EN.md (THIRD_PARTY_EN.md).

Packaged app settings and logs are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%/Kariuss Max Headtracking. These files are excluded from the repository. Redact personal information from screenshots or logs before posting an issue.

To upgrade manually, quit the running app, extract the new Windows ZIP into a new folder, and open its executable. Preferences in the location above are reused.
